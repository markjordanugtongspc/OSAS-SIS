## <a name="getting-started"></a>1. Getting Started

### Login
To access the system, use your assigned credentials (username and password). 
*   Ensure you have the correct permissions for the modules you need to access.
*   If you forget your password, contact the system administrator.

---

## <a name="navigation"></a>2. Navigation

The system uses a **Sidebar Navigation** on the left side of the screen. The sidebar is divided into two main sections:

1.  **Sports**: Contains all modules related to sports equipment inventory and borrowing.
2.  **Storage**: Contains modules for managing office supplies, documents, and cabinets.

*   **Toggle Sidebar**: You can collapse/expand the sidebar using the arrow icon at the top of the sidebar.
*   **Active Page**: The current page is highlighted in the sidebar.

---

## <a name="sports-equipment-management"></a>3. Sports Equipment Management

This module is dedicated to tracking sports gear, managing inventory, and handling borrowing/returns.

### <a name="sports-dashboard"></a>Dashboard
The **Dashboard** gives a quick overview of the current status of sports equipment.
*   **Key Metrics**: View total items, available stock, borrowed items, and overdue returns.
*   **Charts**: Visual representations of inventory distribution and usage.

### <a name="item-inventory"></a>Item Inventory
This page allows you to view the list of available sports equipment.
*   **Search**: Use the search bar to find specific items.
*   **View Details**: Click on an item to see more details, such as quantity and condition.

### <a name="item-management"></a>Item Management
*(Admin Access Only)*
Use this page to add, edit, or delete items from the inventory.
*   **Add Item**: Click the "Add Item" button to register new equipment. You will need to provide details like name, category, and initial quantity.
*   **Edit/Delete**: Managers can update item details or remove obsolete items.

### <a name="borrowing-system"></a>Borrowing System
The core feature for daily operations.
*   **Borrow**: Record a new transaction when a student or staff member borrows equipment.
    *   Select the item and quantity.
    *   Enter the borrower's details.
    *   Set the due date/time.
*   **Return**: Mark items as returned. The system updates the inventory count automatically.

### <a name="history--logs"></a>History & Logs
*   **History**: View a record of all past borrow/return transactions. useful for tracking usage patterns.
*   **Logs**: A detailed audit trail of all actions taken within the system (e.g., "Item Added", "Item Updated"), ensuring accountability.

## <a name="storage--document-management-cms"></a>4. Storage & Document Management (CMS)

This module (also referred to as CMS) handles the organization of physical storage units (cabinets) and the documents or supplies contained within them.

### <a name="storage-dashboard"></a>Dashboard
Similar to the Sports Dashboard, this provides an overview of your storage.
*   **Stats**: Total supplies/equipment, number of cabinets, pending actions, and archived files.
*   **Recent Documents**: A quick list of recently added or modified items.

### <a name="supplies--equipment"></a>Supplies & Equipment
This is the main view for managing your stored items.
*   **File/Item Management**: Add new documents or supplies to the system.
*   **Categorization**: Assign items to specific types and categories for easy filtering.
*   **Search**: Quickly locate documents or supplies across all cabinets.

### <a name="cabinet-management"></a>Cabinet Management
*   **Cabinets**: Create digital representations of your physical cabinets.
*   **Organization**: Assign items to specific cabinets to keep track of their physical location.
*   **Service Association**: Link cabinets and items to specific OSAS services (e.g., "Scholarship", "Guidance").

## <a name="common-features"></a>5. Common Features

### <a name="notifications"></a>Notifications
*   The **Bell Icon** in the sidebar alerts you to important events, such as low stock warnings, overdue borrows, or pending approval requests.
*   Click "Notifications" to view the full list.
*   **Badge**: A red badge indicates the number of unread notifications.

### <a name="profile-management"></a>Profile Management
*   **User Info**: Your name and position are displayed at the bottom of the sidebar.
*   **Edit Profile**: Click the "Edit" icon (pencil) next to your name to update your profile picture, first name, or last name.
*   **Logout**: Securely sign out of the system using the Logout button.
